\plannedsection{Policy Interaction and Constraints}\label{sec:policy-interaction}

\subsection{Fiscal and monetary regimes}
Study how the fiscal backing of public liabilities interacts with interest-rate
policy, inflation, and equilibrium determination.

\subsection{Debt sustainability and policy space}
Derive debt-to-output dynamics and distinguish arithmetic from an assessment of
sustainability. Include growth, interest rates, primary balances, and risk.

\subsection{Policy combinations in an open economy}
Bring together exchange-rate regimes, capital flows, domestic balance sheets,
and international spillovers. Compare policy combinations under stated
institutional and financial constraints.
