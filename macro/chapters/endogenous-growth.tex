\plannedsection{Endogenous Growth and Resource Allocation}\label{sec:endogenous-growth}

\subsection{The AK benchmark and human capital}
Ask when reproducible factors can sustain growth without exogenous technological
progress. Compare the roles of returns to capital and human-capital accumulation.

\subsection{Ideas, innovation, and incentives}
Introduce nonrival ideas and incentives to innovate. Separate the private return
to innovation from its social return and discuss the policy implications.

\subsection{Allocation and aggregate productivity}
Explain how differences in productivity and financing conditions affect the
allocation of capital. Connect allocation to aggregate output and growth.
