\part{Growth}\label{part:growth}

How does an economy's productive capacity evolve? Growth facts lead to
Harrod and Domar's demand and investment questions, then to Solow's capital
adjustment. We move from a given saving rate to optimal saving, overlapping
generations, and endogenous technological change. A closing synthesis connects
the mechanisms to development, demographic change, and the diagnosis of a
growth slowdown before we turn to fluctuations.
