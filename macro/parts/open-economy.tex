\part{Open Economy}\label{part:open-economy}

Opening an economy changes which transactions can cross its analysis boundary.
We first trade one good across dates, separating domestic resources from
residents' spending. We then add different goods and relative prices, before
studying international shocks, exchange rates, and policy regimes. The final
chapter will connect the dynamic models with IS--LM--BP and Mundell--Fleming,
before the broader policy comparisons in Part~\ref{part:policy}.
